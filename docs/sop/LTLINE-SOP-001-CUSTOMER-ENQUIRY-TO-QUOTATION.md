# LTLINE SOP-001 — Customer Enquiry to Quotation

**ID:** LTLINE-SOP-001 | **Revision:** 1.0 | **Status:** DRAFT

## Purpose
Create consistent, technically accurate and commercially controlled quotations.

## Procedure
1. Register the enquiry and assign a reference.
2. Record customer, site, product/service, quantities and deadline.
3. Identify missing technical information.
4. Perform site survey when required.
5. Select approved products or define a project solution.
6. Obtain supplier pricing where needed.
7. Calculate direct and landed costs.
8. Apply approved margin and commercial terms.
9. Prepare quotation with scope, exclusions, warranty, delivery and payment terms.
10. Obtain required internal approval.
11. Issue quotation and record validity.
12. Follow up and record outcome.

## Quality Gate
No quotation may present unverified technical claims as certified specifications.

## Records
Enquiry record, site survey, supplier quotations, costing sheet, approved quotation and customer response.
