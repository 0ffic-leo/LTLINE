# LTLINE SOP-006 — Warranty and After-Sales

**ID:** LTLINE-SOP-006 | **Revision:** 1.0 | **Status:** DRAFT

## Procedure
1. Register service/warranty request.
2. Confirm customer, project/product and warranty status.
3. Classify urgency and safety impact.
4. Collect evidence: photos, serial/model, symptoms and operating conditions.
5. Perform remote diagnosis where appropriate.
6. Schedule site intervention when required.
7. Determine cause: installation, product, operation, wear, external event or undetermined.
8. Apply approved corrective action.
9. Record parts, labour and findings.
10. Obtain customer confirmation where appropriate.
11. Close the case and record lessons learned.

## Safety Escalation
Potential electrical, fire, gas/refrigerant, structural or other serious hazards must be escalated immediately and the equipment isolated where necessary and safe to do so.

## Records
Service request, warranty assessment, service report, photographs, parts used and closure confirmation.
