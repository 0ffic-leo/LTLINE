# LTLINE SOP-002 — Procurement

**ID:** LTLINE-SOP-002 | **Revision:** 1.0 | **Status:** DRAFT

## Procedure
1. Confirm approved requirement.
2. Identify approved or candidate suppliers.
3. Request comparable quotations.
4. Verify specification, model, quantity, lead time and warranty.
5. Compare total landed cost, not purchase price alone.
6. Select supplier according to technical and commercial criteria.
7. Obtain purchase approval.
8. Issue purchase order.
9. Confirm supplier acceptance and delivery date.
10. Receive and inspect goods.
11. Record discrepancies and corrective actions.
12. Match purchase order, delivery and invoice before payment approval.

## Records
RFQ, quotation comparison, approval, purchase order, delivery record, inspection record and supplier invoice.
