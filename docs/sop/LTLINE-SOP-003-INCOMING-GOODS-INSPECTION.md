# LTLINE SOP-003 — Incoming Goods Inspection

**ID:** LTLINE-SOP-003 | **Revision:** 1.0 | **Status:** DRAFT

## Procedure
1. Identify delivery against purchase order.
2. Check packaging and visible transport damage.
3. Verify item, model, quantity and basic specification.
4. Check required documentation and certificates where applicable.
5. Photograph significant damage or discrepancies.
6. Mark each delivery as accepted, accepted with deviation, quarantined or rejected.
7. Notify procurement and supplier of discrepancies.
8. Record corrective action and final disposition.

## Hold Rule
Products with material uncertainty affecting safety, specification or customer requirements must not be released for project use until resolved.

## Records
Delivery note, inspection checklist, photographs, non-conformity record and supplier communication.
