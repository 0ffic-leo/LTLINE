# LTLINE Export Readiness Framework

**Document ID:** LTLINE-DOC-09  
**Revision:** 1.0  
**Status:** DRAFT

## 1. Purpose

This framework prepares LTLINE for future international sales without treating export activity as established before the required legal, technical and commercial controls are in place.

## 2. Export Readiness Areas

### Legal and Corporate

- Appropriate legal structure
- Tax and customs registration requirements
- Contracts and terms of sale
- Product responsibility

### Product Compliance

- Correct product identification
- Manufacturer documentation
- Applicable conformity requirements
- Technical files where required
- User / installation documentation

### Packaging and Labelling

- Product identification
- Quantity and unit information
- Handling information
- Country-of-origin information where required
- Customer / shipment references

### Logistics

- Incoterms where appropriate
- Freight planning
- Customs documentation
- Commercial invoice
- Packing list
- Transport documents
- Insurance considerations

### Commercial

- Export price calculation
- Landed-cost analysis
- Currency management
- Payment terms
- Customer credit assessment

## 3. Target Market Evaluation

Before entering a new market, LTLINE should evaluate:

- Market demand
- Competition
- Applicable product requirements
- Import duties / taxes
- Logistics cost
- Distribution options
- Customer support requirements
- Currency and payment risks

## 4. Export Product Pack

Products selected for export should have a controlled documentation package containing, as applicable:

- Product datasheet
- Technical specifications
- Certificates / declarations
- Installation instructions
- Warranty terms
- Packaging specification
- HS / customs classification information where validated
- Export pricing model

## 5. Export Readiness Gate

A product should only be designated `EXPORT READY` after the responsible team has verified the applicable legal, technical, documentation, packaging and logistics requirements for the target market.

## 6. Development Principle

Export capability will be developed progressively. The Kosovo domestic operating system remains the foundation from which international processes are standardized.
