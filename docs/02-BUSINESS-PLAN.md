# LTLINE Business Plan Framework

**Document ID:** LTLINE-DOC-02  
**Revision:** 1.0  
**Status:** DRAFT

## 1. Purpose

This document provides the framework for the LTLINE business plan. Detailed financial projections, market research and product-level costing will be added as validated data becomes available.

## 2. Business Model

LTLINE is planned around a combination of:

- Product sales
- Technical supply
- Installation services
- Project-based construction and modular solutions
- HVAC and ventilation projects
- PIR panel and insulation solutions
- Future export-oriented product sales

## 3. Investment Framework

The current planning assumption is an initial investment of **€100,000**. The final allocation should be divided into clearly controlled categories such as equipment, inventory, premises, vehicles/logistics, working capital, marketing and contingency.

No category allocation should be treated as final until supported by actual quotations or approved management assumptions.

## 4. Revenue Streams

### 4.1 Product Revenue

Revenue generated from the sale of standardized products and components.

### 4.2 Installation Revenue

Revenue generated from technical installation and commissioning.

### 4.3 Project Revenue

Revenue from complete or partial delivery of modular homes, villas and related technical systems.

### 4.4 Service Revenue

Maintenance, inspection, troubleshooting and after-sales services.

## 5. Cost Structure

The financial model should separately track:

- Direct materials
- Imported products
- Transport and customs-related costs
- Labour
- Installation costs
- Premises
- Utilities
- Vehicles and logistics
- Tools and equipment
- Marketing and sales
- Administration
- Taxes and statutory costs
- Warranty / contingency provisions

## 6. Key Performance Indicators

The final financial model should monitor at minimum:

- Monthly revenue
- Gross margin
- Operating expenses
- Net operating result
- Cash flow
- Inventory value
- Receivables
- Payables
- Project profitability
- Customer acquisition cost

## 7. Growth Phases

### Phase 1 — Foundation

Establish the legal, operational, technical and documentation base in Kosovo.

### Phase 2 — Market Development

Build recurring local sales, supplier relationships and project references.

### Phase 3 — Product Standardization

Convert successful solutions into repeatable product and project packages.

### Phase 4 — Export Readiness

Prepare documentation, packaging, compliance and logistics processes for international sales.

## 8. Financial Control Principle

Every financial figure must be classified as one of:

- `ASSUMPTION`
- `QUOTE`
- `ACTUAL`
- `FORECAST`

This prevents planning values from being confused with real operating results.
