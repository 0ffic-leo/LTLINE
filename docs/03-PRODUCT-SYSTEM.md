# LTLINE Product System

**Document ID:** LTLINE-DOC-03  
**Revision:** 1.0  
**Status:** DRAFT

## 1. Purpose

The LTLINE Product System defines a common structure for identifying, specifying, purchasing, selling, installing and supporting LTLINE products and technical solutions.

## 2. Product Families

### LTLINE-HVAC

Climate and HVAC equipment, components and complete climate solutions.

### LTLINE-VENT

Ventilation equipment, ducting, air-distribution components, accessories and complete ventilation systems.

### LTLINE-MOD

Modular construction systems, modular homes and associated structural and technical packages.

### LTLINE-VILLA

Integrated technical packages for villas and residential construction projects.

### LTLINE-PIR

PIR insulation panels and related thermal-envelope solutions.

## 3. Product Master Record

Each standardized product should have a product master record containing:

- Product ID
- Product name
- Product family
- Manufacturer / supplier
- Model or part number
- Technical specification
- Unit of measure
- Purchase cost
- Landed cost where applicable
- Target selling price
- Warranty information
- Applicable certificates / declarations
- Installation requirements
- Maintenance requirements
- Revision date

## 4. Technical Data Hierarchy

Technical information should be maintained in the following order of authority:

1. Manufacturer documentation
2. Applicable standards and regulatory requirements
3. Approved LTLINE technical specification
4. Supplier quotation / commercial documentation
5. Internal working assumptions

Internal assumptions must never be presented to a customer as certified manufacturer specifications.

## 5. Product Status

- `CONCEPT` — proposed product or solution
- `EVALUATION` — technical/commercial assessment in progress
- `APPROVED` — authorized for sale or project use
- `ACTIVE` — currently offered
- `SUSPENDED` — temporarily unavailable or under review
- `DISCONTINUED` — no longer offered

## 6. Pricing Structure

Product pricing should distinguish:

**Purchase Cost → Landed Cost → Operational Cost → Margin → Selling Price**

For imported products, landed cost may include transport, customs, duties, VAT treatment where relevant, handling and other directly attributable costs.

## 7. Product Documentation Pack

An approved product should have, where applicable:

- Product datasheet
- Technical drawing
- Installation guide
- Operating / maintenance information
- Warranty terms
- Compliance documents
- Product photographs
- Packaging information
- Spare-parts information

## 8. Project Solutions

When LTLINE sells a complete technical solution rather than a single product, the solution should receive a project or package identifier and contain a bill of materials, technical scope, exclusions, installation requirements and commissioning requirements.

## 9. Change Control

Any material change to a product's model, supplier, technical specification, certification, warranty or commercial terms should trigger a product record review and revision where necessary.
