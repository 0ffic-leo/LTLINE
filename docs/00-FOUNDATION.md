# LTLINE Foundation Framework

**Document ID:** LTLINE-DOC-00  
**Revision:** 1.0  
**Status:** DRAFT

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the foundational architecture of the LTLINE documentation system. It establishes how the company's business, technical, operational, quality, financial and project documents are organized and controlled.

## 2. Scope

The framework covers the complete operating cycle:

1. Strategy and business planning
2. Product definition
3. Procurement and supplier management
4. Sales and quotations
5. Project planning
6. Installation and execution
7. Quality and safety control
8. Handover and after-sales service
9. Financial monitoring
10. Future export readiness

## 3. Core Business Areas

LTLINE's current foundation scope includes:

- Ventilation systems
- Climate / HVAC solutions
- Modular homes
- Villas and residential technical solutions
- PIR insulation panel systems
- Related technical installation and project services

## 4. Document Hierarchy

### Level 1 — Corporate

Company profile, strategy, governance and business plan.

### Level 2 — Management Systems

Quality, HSE, procurement, sales, finance and project management.

### Level 3 — Technical

Products, specifications, installation methods, testing and commissioning.

### Level 4 — Operational Templates

Forms, checklists, quotations, project records, inspection records and handover documents.

## 5. Control Rules

Every controlled document should contain:

- Document title
- Document ID
- Revision number
- Status
- Owner / responsible function
- Effective date when approved
- Revision history

## 6. Development Roadmap

The Foundation Pack will be developed in the following order:

1. Company profile
2. Business plan
3. Product system
4. SOP system
5. Quality and HSE
6. Project management
7. Sales and procurement
8. Financial framework
9. Export readiness
10. Document control

## 7. Approval Principle

A document remains `DRAFT` until it has been reviewed and explicitly approved. Approved documents become the operational reference version until superseded by a newer approved revision.
