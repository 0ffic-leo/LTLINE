# LTLINE Quality, Health, Safety and Environment

**Document ID:** LTLINE-DOC-05  
**Revision:** 1.0  
**Status:** DRAFT

## 1. Purpose

LTLINE's Quality, Health, Safety and Environment framework defines minimum controls for professional and responsible project execution.

## 2. Quality Principles

LTLINE aims to:

- Deliver according to the approved technical scope
- Use verified products and materials
- Inspect incoming materials
- Record critical installation checks
- Test and commission systems where applicable
- Document non-conformities and corrective actions
- Maintain traceability of important project records

## 3. Quality Gates

Projects should use quality gates at appropriate stages:

1. Design / scope review
2. Procurement verification
3. Incoming material inspection
4. Installation inspection
5. Testing and commissioning
6. Final handover

## 4. Health and Safety

Work activities must be planned with appropriate attention to:

- Personal protective equipment
- Electrical safety
- Work at height
- Lifting and handling
- Tools and machinery
- Fire prevention
- Site access and housekeeping
- Safe storage of materials
- Emergency response

The exact controls must be adapted to the work activity and applicable Kosovo requirements.

## 5. Environmental Principles

LTLINE should minimize avoidable waste and environmental impact through:

- Efficient material planning
- Responsible waste segregation
- Proper disposal of construction waste
- Energy-conscious equipment selection
- Avoidance of unnecessary packaging waste where practical

## 6. Non-Conformity

A non-conformity should be recorded when a product, installation, process or deliverable fails to meet an approved requirement.

The record should identify:

- What happened
- Where it occurred
- Responsible process / supplier
- Immediate containment
- Root cause where appropriate
- Corrective action
- Verification of closure

## 7. Incident Reporting

Safety incidents, near misses and significant property or environmental events should be documented and reviewed so that preventive actions can be implemented.

## 8. Responsibility

Quality and HSE responsibilities should be assigned before project execution and communicated to workers and relevant subcontractors.
