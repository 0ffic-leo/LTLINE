# LTLINE SOP-001 — Customer Enquiry to Quotation

**Document ID:** LTLINE-SOP-001  
**Revision:** 1.0  
**Status:** DRAFT  
**Owner:** Sales / Technical

## 1. Purpose

Standardize the process from first customer contact through preparation and internal approval of a quotation.

## 2. Procedure

1. Register the enquiry.
2. Identify customer and project requirements.
3. Determine whether a site survey is required.
4. Collect drawings, dimensions, photographs and technical information where applicable.
5. Identify the required products / solution.
6. Request supplier pricing when needed.
7. Calculate direct and landed costs.
8. Add labour, logistics and other direct costs.
9. Apply the approved commercial margin.
10. Prepare quotation with scope, exclusions, warranty and payment terms.
11. Review technical accuracy.
12. Obtain required commercial approval.
13. Issue quotation and record validity.
14. Track customer response.

## 3. Mandatory Records

- Customer information
- Site survey where applicable
- Supplier quotations
- Costing calculation
- Customer quotation
- Approval record

## 4. Controls

No quotation should contain unverified technical claims. Supplier pricing and internal assumptions must be distinguishable in the costing record.

## 5. Output

Approved customer quotation and traceable sales record.
