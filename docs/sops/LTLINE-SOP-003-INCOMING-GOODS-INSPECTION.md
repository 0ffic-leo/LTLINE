# LTLINE SOP-003 — Incoming Goods Inspection

**Document ID:** LTLINE-SOP-003  
**Revision:** 1.0  
**Status:** DRAFT  
**Owner:** Warehouse / Technical

## 1. Purpose

Verify received materials and products before they are released for storage, installation or sale.

## 2. Inspection

Check:

- Supplier and purchase order
- Product/model/part number
- Quantity
- Packaging condition
- Visible damage
- Required accessories
- Technical identification
- Certificates / documentation where required
- Serial / batch numbers where applicable

## 3. Status

Received goods should be marked or recorded as:

- `ACCEPTED`
- `ACCEPTED WITH OBSERVATION`
- `QUARANTINED`
- `REJECTED`

## 4. Non-Conformity

If a discrepancy is identified, preserve evidence, notify the responsible person and record the issue before the material is used where use could create technical, warranty or safety risk.

## 5. Records

Inspection checklist, photographs where useful, supplier correspondence and non-conformity record.
