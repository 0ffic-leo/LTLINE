# LTLINE SOP-002 — Procurement

**Document ID:** LTLINE-SOP-002  
**Revision:** 1.0  
**Status:** DRAFT  
**Owner:** Procurement / Management

## 1. Purpose

Ensure purchases are technically suitable, commercially controlled and traceable.

## 2. Procedure

1. Receive approved purchase requirement.
2. Confirm specification, quantity and required date.
3. Identify approved or potential suppliers.
4. Request and collect quotations where appropriate.
5. Compare price, technical compliance, lead time, warranty and terms.
6. Select supplier according to approved criteria.
7. Obtain required purchase approval.
8. Issue purchase order.
9. Confirm order and delivery schedule.
10. Track shipment / delivery.
11. Inspect received goods.
12. Record discrepancies.
13. Accept or quarantine material.
14. Match invoice against approved purchase documentation.

## 3. Controls

Urgent purchases must still be documented retrospectively. Supplier substitutions require technical and commercial review when they could affect the approved scope.

## 4. Records

- Purchase request
- Supplier RFQ
- Supplier quotations
- Comparison
- Purchase order
- Delivery record
- Inspection record
- Invoice

## 5. Output

Approved purchase with traceable supplier and receiving records.
