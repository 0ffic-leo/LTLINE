# LTLINE Company Profile

**Document ID:** LTLINE-DOC-01  
**Revision:** 1.0  
**Status:** DRAFT

## 1. Identity

**Brand:** LTLINE  
**Base of operations:** Skenderaj, Kosovo  
**Initial legal form:** Business Individual (B.I.)

LTLINE is being developed as a technical and project-oriented business providing integrated solutions in construction-related technical systems, climate and ventilation, modular buildings and insulation technologies.

## 2. Vision

To develop LTLINE into a reliable technical solutions provider capable of delivering complete, professionally documented projects from concept and procurement through installation, commissioning and after-sales support.

## 3. Mission

Provide practical, technically sound and commercially competitive solutions while maintaining consistent quality, documented procedures and responsible project execution.

## 4. Core Activities

- Ventilation and air-management systems
- Climate / HVAC systems
- Modular homes
- Villas and residential projects
- PIR panel solutions
- Technical installation and project coordination
- Related procurement and supplier coordination

## 5. Initial Operating Framework

The current project planning framework assumes:

- Initial investment target: €100,000
- Rented operating premises
- Initial workforce assumption: 4 workers

These figures are planning assumptions and must be updated when the actual business structure is finalized.

## 6. Market Direction

The initial commercial focus is Kosovo. The documentation system is designed from the beginning so that products, projects and records can later be adapted for international and export markets.

## 7. Positioning

LTLINE should compete through:

- Integrated technical solutions
- Professional documentation
- Transparent quotations
- Reliable installation
- Quality control
- Project accountability
- After-sales support

## 8. Organizational Development

The organization will be expanded as activity grows. Roles should be defined by responsibility and process rather than by job title alone, with clear ownership for sales, procurement, technical execution, quality, finance and administration.
